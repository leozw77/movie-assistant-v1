DoubanReview 0.9.0-preview.6 BuildFix11 R1
Date: 2026-08-05

Implemented:
1. Two long-lived Douban WebView2 controls sharing one CoreWebView2Environment/Profile.
2. DetailWebView owns subject metadata/home-cast navigation.
3. WorkerWebView owns official review reads/writes, full cast, person-avatar fallback, history and search.
4. Single-consumer priority Worker queue: save=0, official read=1, full cast=2, avatar=3, background=4.
5. Save jobs become non-cancelable once official submission begins; lower-priority avatar jobs are cancelable.
6. SubjectId + RequestId stale-result protection in C# and JavaScript.
7. Home cast reads the first six Douban cards without person-level deduplication.
8. Full cast is card-scoped to li.celebrity and uses complete-state stable sampling.
9. Unified person-avatar URL validation and persistent 90-day PersonAvatarCache.
10. Incremental castAvatarUpdated messages for background avatar completion.
11. CastParserVersion=2 and FullCastParserVersion=2 invalidate only old cast caches.
12. WebView role, JobId, RequestId, priority, timing and duplicate-avatar diagnostics.
13. Process-failure recovery is isolated per Douban WebView2 controller.
14. Existing review transaction semantics remain unchanged; deletion remains disabled.
