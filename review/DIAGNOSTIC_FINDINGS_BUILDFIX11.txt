BuildFix11 findings and implemented corrections
Date: 2026-08-05

1. Previous architecture used one Douban WebView2 for metadata, full cast, person pages and review transactions. Navigation-heavy tasks serialized and displaced the detail page.
   Correction: DetailWebView and WorkerWebView are long-lived and share the same CoreWebView2Environment/Profile.

2. Home-cast extraction deduplicated by profile/person identity, removing a repeated card when one person appeared in multiple jobs.
   Correction: card-order extraction keeps up to six official cards and performs no person-level deduplication.

3. Full-cast ancestor scoring could select a shared parent and assign one card's first image to a neighboring person.
   Correction: direct li.celebrity traversal; image extraction is restricted to the current card.

4. Full-cast extraction returned on the first complete-looking sample before lazy avatars stabilized.
   Correction: readyState complete plus consecutive card/avatar signature stability at 250 ms intervals.

5. Bad Cast/FullCast results could stay cached for seven days.
   Correction: parser versions are upgraded to 2 and only Cast/FullCast fields/timestamps are invalidated.

6. Background avatar work could block review writes.
   Correction: a single-consumer priority queue preempts cancelable low-priority jobs; an official submission is non-cancelable once started.

7. Old async results could arrive after switching subjects.
   Correction: SubjectId + RequestId validation in both C# and JavaScript; stale results may persist to their own cache but cannot update the current UI.

8. Missing/placeholder avatar checks were fragmented.
   Correction: BrowserCdpService.IsValidPersonAvatarUrl is the shared gate and PersonAvatarCache stores only accepted URLs.
